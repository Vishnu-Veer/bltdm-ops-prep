# BLTDM Ops Prep

A study site for DECA **Business Law and Ethics Team Decision Making**, focused on **Operations**.

- **Practice case**: draws a made-up business problem with 7 PIs, and has a 30-minute prep timer and a 15-minute presentation timer.
- **Learn**: a lesson path through all 64 Operations PIs, with 5 question types, hearts, XP and a daily streak.
- **PI checklist**: the full official list. What you tick saves on your device.
- **Game plan**: the 5 steps for answering any case.

PIs are from DECA's 2026–27 HS Business Management and Administration list (MBA Research). Practice cases are made up for studying.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages link.
