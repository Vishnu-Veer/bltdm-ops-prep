# BLTDM Ops Prep

A study site for DECA **Business Law and Ethics Team Decision Making**, focused on **Operations**. Three tabs:

- **Definitions**: 99 law, ethics and operations terms, A–Z and searchable, each tagged with the PIs it helps you hit.
- **PIs**: all 64 Operations PIs from DECA's 2026–27 list. Tap one for what it means, how to use it in a case, a line to say, and vocab to drop.
- **Practice**: pick the response that would score higher, and every 4th question write your own response. Written answers are graded by Gemini AI (paste a free key under "AI grading", stored only on your device), with an offline check as backup.

The iPhone app (`ios/`) bundles the site for offline use. `sh ios/install-device.sh` builds it and installs it on a plugged-in iPhone; it reads your key from `.env` (git-ignored) so AI grading works without pasting.
